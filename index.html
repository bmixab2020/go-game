Learn Go Weiqi / Baduk

Welcome to Go

Go is a two-player strategy board game that began in China more than 2,500 years ago. It is known as Weiqi in China, Baduk in Korea and Igo in Japan.

Players take turns placing black and white stones on the intersections of a grid. Stones never move, but a stone or group with no free neighboring points (liberties) is captured and removed. The goal is to surround more of the board than your opponent.

The rules fit in a few sentences, yet the strategy is so deep that Go has been called the most complex board game ever played. You can learn the rules in about fifteen minutes.

    Quick rules

    • Take turns placing stones.
    • Surround stones to capture them.
    • No suicide, and no immediate ko recapture.
    • Two passes in a row end the game.
    • Most points (stones + territory) wins. White gets komi.
